Privacy Policy of the North Giza Diocese Courses App

  • We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, including servants, attendees, and course participants, outlining how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information within the North Giza Diocese Courses application.
  • We collect basic personal data such as full names, phone numbers, email addresses, and educational or service stages to handle account creation and course management effectively.
  • We track account and progress data including login credentials, enrolled courses, quiz or exam scores, and lecture completion rates.
  • We gather technical usage and device data such as device types, operating systems, and IP addresses to optimize application performance and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • We use collected data strictly for creating and managing user accounts, facilitating secure logins, and tracking attendance.
  • We administer course materials, conduct exams, and issue certificates under the direct supervision of the diocese.
  • We communicate with users regarding schedule updates, course announcements, and administrative or church notices.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade personal data to external commercial or advertising entities under any circumstances.
  • We share registration and attendance data exclusively with authorized course administrators and servants responsible for educational and administrative supervision within the North Giza Diocese for church and service purposes only.
  • We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction using secure and encrypted servers.
  • We retain personal data only as long as user accounts remain active or as necessary to provide educational services, allowing users to request account and data deletion at any time.
  • Users retain the right to access, update, or correct their registered personal data and opt out of non-essential notifications or broadcast messages.
  • We ensure that data for younger age groups or Sunday school programs is collected strictly under the supervision and consent of parents or officially designated service leaders.
  • We may update this privacy policy periodically, posting any modifications on this page with an updated revision date, where continued app use implies acceptance.
  • Users can reach out with questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy through the official email and communication channels provided by the diocese.
